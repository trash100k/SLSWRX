# SalesWorx: Growth + Product Elevation Specification

*Principal product/systems design spec — July 2026. Companion to the Voice, Scoring, Ranking, and Infra tracks; cites codebase audit (A) and verified research (B) throughout.*

---

## 1. Positioning

**SalesWorx is the first competitive sales sport — "chess.com of sales calls" — and nobody can follow us there.** Every funded competitor monetizes enablement budgets: Hyperbound ($18.3M, Peak XV) gates "Internal Gamification" behind its enterprise plan and runs only episodic marketing contests ($300–$4K prize pools, no persistent ladder); Second Nature ($38M) doesn't work below ~15 seats and its most-documented complaint is inconsistent AI scoring; Nooks ($70M) buries roleplay inside a $5K/yr dialer; Gong charges $40–60K year one to score calls after the fact. The structural consequence of selling to VP-Enablement is private data, bland HR-safe feedback, sales-led pricing, and internal-only leaderboards — which means **no incumbent can ship a public cross-company Elo ladder, a one-attempt high-stakes mode, or brutal roast-clip content without breaking their buyer** (competitors research, verified: zero products in market have any of the three, and "chess.com of sales" positioning is unclaimed as of July 2026). Meanwhile the bottom-up demand is proven: 40K+ reps used Hyperbound's free demo bots, #coldcalling roleplay is a documented TikTok format (16.3M-view top clip), and the "cold call with me" trend is live. SalesWorx claims the Duolingo-vs-Rosetta-Stone opening: free voice roleplay against AI buyers who hang up on you, a Roast Master whose verdicts are entertainment, a real Glicko-2 ranked ladder that makes your number a status object on LinkedIn, and a weekly One Shot everyone in sales faces together. Individual SDRs, AEs, job seekers, and sales students play free; their managers buy the team layer later. The loop is **Call → Roast → Rank → Share** — everything else in the app serves it or gets cut.

## 2. Twenty Elevations of What Exists

*(These deepen current surfaces. Effort: S = days, M = 1–2 weeks, L = 3+ weeks.)*

1. **Live voice in the Arena via Gemini Live (native audio).** Replace the Web Speech dictation hack (Arena.tsx:35–82) with a real speech-to-speech call: server mints a config-locked ephemeral token (`client.authTokens.create`, `liveConnectConstraints` pinning `gemini-2.5-flash-native-audio-preview-12-2025`), browser connects direct via the `@google/genai` SDK already in the stack. Voice is the 2026 baseline — Hyperbound set sub-second latency as the realism bar — and at ~$0.06–0.09 per 5-min call it's ~10x cheaper than OpenAI, the only economics that survive freemium (voice-stack verification). **L**

2. **Give every boss the power to hang up on you.** Declare a `hang_up_call(reason, finalLine)` tool on the voice session; on trigger, play a click SFX, kill the session, route to PostCall with the reason stamped on the grade. Hyperbound's bots that "hang up on you, get angry, or curse you out" are its most-cited emotional spike, and "HUNG UP ON at 0:47" is the single most shareable failure artifact this game can produce (viral-growth research). **S** (on top of #1)

3. **Real Glicko-2, server-side, replacing `(score−70)×2`.** Kill the fake delta in game.ts:43–50; players and bosses both become rated entities (Lichess puzzle model: player 1500/RD 350, bosses seeded by difficulty tier at RD 300), outcome discretized W/D/L so a prompt-injected 100 pays no more than an honest 78. A ladder is only status-worthy if it's credible and unfakeable — localStorage Elo is decorative (ranking-systems research). Requires auth + Postgres (infra track). **L**

4. **Scoring-trust overhaul of the Roast Master.** Move the 10 metrics to 0–4 anchored bands with calibration exemplars, N=3 ensemble median on gemini-3-flash-preview (~$0.04/grade blended), verbatim evidence quotes fuzzy-verified in code (≥0.9 match nullifies unproven metrics), versioned judge prompts with a golden-set CI gate. "Judges the same content differently each time" is the category's #1 trust complaint (Second Nature G2 reviews) — consistency IS the moat that lets the roast be brutal without being dismissed as random. **L**

5. **Fix the five integration bugs that break trust today.** (a) Arena honors `?ranked=true`; (b) PostCall grades custom bosses against the actual custom boss, not Greg (PostCall.tsx:75); (c) follow-up score renders /100 not "/10" (line 318) and calls the transcript-aware `/api/followup`; (d) fetch failures get retry UI instead of `console.error` swallows; (e) fix TrainingGrounds' missing `Activity` import crash. These are silent product-killers a new user hits in session one. **S**

6. **Persist every call: transcripts, grades, sentiment series, ELO deltas to Postgres.** Today transcripts die in router state and grades evaporate after render (audit, cross-cutting #6) — meaning FilmRoom, match history, trend charts, and One Shot literally have no data substrate. This single migration makes ~6 mock screens (Dashboard charts, RankedHub history, FilmRoom, Profile) honest instead of fabricated. **M**

7. **Generated boss openers + boss speaks first, in character, in voice.** Kill the hardcoded per-bossId opening lines (Arena.tsx:111–119) — a generated custom boss currently always opens "I only have two minutes." Have the persona model generate the cold-open from persona + scenario + a daily seed, so no two calls start identically and replays feel alive. **S**

8. **Sidecar sentiment + whisper coach decoupled from the voice path.** Stream finalized transcript turns to gemini-3.1-flash-lite ($0.25/1M) for the 0–100 sentiment and Scan Enemy hints, exactly like today's text contract but firing deterministically every turn — never trust the voice model to call a sentiment tool reliably (voice-stack recommendation). Keeps the HP bar honest in voice mode and keeps coach hints out of the buyer's context. **M**

9. **Deterministic metrics panel: the "provably fair" 40%.** Compute talk-listen ratio (43–57% target band), question count (peak 11–16, penalize >20 — Gong's 326K-call data), longest monologue (≤2:30), WPM (150 sweet spot), filler-word rate, interruption count from timestamps — pure code, $0, undisputable. Publish the formulas in-app; this is both the anti-gaming backbone and the feature that makes reps trust the roast (scoring-judge research). **M**

10. **PostCall as a reveal, not a form.** Score counts up with SFX, metrics flip one-by-one with the evidence quote surfacing on tap, the roast line types out, and the ELO delta lands with a rank-progress animation keyed to real Glicko movement. The grade is the game's dopamine moment; today it renders as a static grid. Display the `improvements` and `rewrite` fields the server already returns and the client throws away (PostCall audit gap 6). **M**

11. **FilmRoom becomes real replay theater.** Wire it to persisted transcripts + server-side recordings (LiveKit egress for ranked, MediaRecorder upload for casual): audio scrubber, key moments pinned on the timeline from `/api/grade`'s keyMoments output (currently discarded), judge annotations inline. This is the Speedrun.com "replay as proof" substrate ranked integrity needs anyway (ranking-systems research). **L**

12. **Onboarding: first call in under 60 seconds, no signup.** Anonymous Supabase session → one tap → you're talking to a demo boss → you get roasted → THEN the signup wall to save your grade and enter ranked. Hyperbound's free public demo is its proven top-of-funnel; we keep the user in a game instead of funneling to a demo booking (competitors research). Also: fix index.html's "My Google AI Studio App" title + zero OG tags — shared links currently unfurl as a blank scaffold, fatal for a viral product. **M**

13. **Make the three arcade mini-games honest with one cheap judge endpoint.** Guillotine, RapidFire, and Negotiation all capture transcripts then award `Math.random()` or scripted outcomes (audit B). One `/api/quick-judge` (flash-lite, single-criterion rubric: opener quality / objection rebuttal / concession analysis) makes all three real, with persisted best-scores feeding leaderboards. Fix the Guillotine stale-closure bug and click-to-win backdoor while there. **M**

14. **Streaks + comeback scaffolding.** Daily-play streak with one earnable freeze per month (Duolingo: 7-day-streak users 3.6x likelier to complete; freezes lift long-term retention ~10%; 10M users hold 365-day streaks), a 48-hour completion window (Chess.com pattern), and a "revenge match" prompt against the last boss that hung up on you. Streaks are the cheapest retention machinery in the research and the app has zero today. **M**

15. **Difficulty curve + placement flow.** 5 placement calls against a fixed calibration ladder of bosses (1100→2000 rating) with predicted rank shown after each (OW2 pattern); post-placement, ranked queue recommends bosses within ±200 of your rating. Kills the current experience of a day-one user getting Procurement Patty (NIGHTMARE) with no ramp; makes RankedHub's fake queue real (ranking-systems research). **M**

16. **Sound design + call ambience.** Client-side WebAudio: looped office/call-center bed under the buyer's track (per-boss ambience — provider-agnostic, free), ring tone on connect, the hang-up click, rank-up sting, roast-reveal sting. Sound is 50% of the "you're on a real call" illusion and costs nothing (voice-stack: skip vendor ambience features, mix client-side). **S**

17. **Roast intensity dial — accessibility of the brutal tone.** Three settings: Professional / Spicy (default) / Scorched Earth, same rubric and scores underneath, only the prose register changes; org/team contexts can pin Professional. Negativity bias (5x psychological weight) makes roast the brand, but an uncalibrated-cruelty complaint on r/sales is a real risk — the dial lets the roast stay savage for those who opted in and defensible everywhere else (viral-growth research). **S**

18. **Mobile-first pass on the core loop.** Arena, PostCall, and share flows must work one-handed on a phone: big push-to-talk affordance, sentiment bar visible while speaking, share sheet native. The TikTok/LinkedIn audience discovers this on mobile; vertical-video output (elevation of clips, feature #7 below) presumes players are already there. Audit shows FF-battle layouts built desktop-only. **M**

19. **Armory actually loads into calls.** Equipped framework (Challenger/MEDDPICC) injects a grading-rubric bias and hint style into `/api/grade` and the whisper coach; battlecards become real objection-intel unlocked by beating the relevant boss. Currently a useState toggle nothing reads (audit). Turns dead cosmetics into a progression system that changes play. **M**

20. **Profile = shareable trophy room fed by real data.** Wire Profile to the real rating, badges, W/L, streak, and per-metric radar aggregated from persisted grades (it currently imports nothing real — audit); make "SHARE STATS" generate an OG card of rank + peak + signature stat. Monkeytype proves skill-number-as-identity sustains communities; the profile is where rank becomes a LinkedIn flex. **M**

## 3. Ten Net-New Features

1. **One Shot Weekly (the flagship).** Every Monday 00:00 UTC one handcrafted boss + scenario drops, identical worldwide; 48-hour window; ONE attempt per verified account, consumed server-side the instant first audio streams (5-min disconnect grace). Percentile leaderboard (global/friends/company), feeds ranked at 1.5x weight, participation streak with monthly freeze. *Why:* the Wordle/Spelunky/GeoGuessr shared-challenge law — comparability is what makes results shareable, and scarcity manufactures the stakes no competitor offers (Mindtickle literally markets unlimited retries). Entry gates (verified email, completed placements, RD < 150) block retry-farming. **Effort: L** (depends on auth/DB/attempt-locking).

2. **Roast Clip Auto-Generator.** Post-call, a pgmq job cuts the most brutal 15–30s (hang-up moment, killer objection, roast verdict read aloud) into a vertical audiogram with captions + score overlay via ffmpeg/Remotion on the worker box (free ≤3-person company); one-tap "Share the carnage." *Why:* no product in the category generates shareable clips despite active #coldcalling TikTok (competitors gap e); losing must be more shareable than winning (5x negativity bias). This is the TikTok/Shorts/Reels supply engine. **L**

3. **Embeddable "Beat Our AI Buyer" Widget + Challenge Links.** A public link/iframe hosting one boss with a public leaderboard; anyone can generate a challenge link from any boss ("I scored 71 vs the Vetoing CFO — your turn"), and companies can embed their own branded buyer on their site. *Why:* the challenge link is the atomic viral unit; the embed turns other companies' websites into acquisition surface. Mirrors Hyperbound's proven free-demo loop but keeps players in our game (competitors research). **M**

4. **Duel-a-Friend (async same-scenario versus).** Send a duel link → both players face the identical boss/scenario/seed → side-by-side scorecards, per-metric winner highlighting, and a "watch their call" replay unlock after you've played. Makes WarRoom's alert()-stub real. *Why:* direct social comparison with a friend is a stronger trigger than a global ladder; the loser's rematch demand is the retention hook; every duel sends at least one invite (K-factor input). **M**

5. **Influencer Boss Drops.** Limited-time, consented AI-likeness bosses of 5–10 names from the Demandbase Top 100 / 30MPC orbit ("Can you get past Jen Allen-Knuth?"), each with its own leaderboard and a 2-week window. *Why:* the Mittens template (40M games, +40% platform volume, record 10M DAU in a month) already validated in THIS market by Hyperbound's Adam Robinson bot, which "booked out three weeks of sales calendars" on a $1K prize — influencers promote their own boss because the leaderboard is a status mirror (viral-growth research). **M** per drop after voice + persistence exist.

6. **Company Wars (domain-based team ladders).** Auto-cluster players by verified email domain into company leaderboards; company vs. company weekly matchups scored by average of top-5 One Shot percentiles; public "Sales Org Power Rankings." *Why:* the Wrapped Party / LinkedIn-connection-leaderboard pattern applied to the identity salespeople actually care about; also the B2B2C wedge — managers discover their team is ranked 47th and ask who sells the team tier (Lavender's bottom-up playbook). **M**

7. **Spectator Theater + Failure Reels.** When a One Shot window closes: top-10 runs become public annotated replays; auto-compiled stats ("87% of reps died at the pricing objection") and a weekly failure montage. Live-spectate marquee duels later via Supabase Realtime channels. *Why:* HQ Trivia's visible elimination count was the drama; Spelunky's daily is a spectator staple; watching others fail the call you failed is irresistible and produces its own content (ranking + viral research). **L**

8. **Boss Workshop (UGC with Glicko ratings).** Rebuild Architect to actually call `/api/generate-boss` (today it's a setTimeout — audit), persist blueprints server-side, publish to a community gallery where every published boss gets played, rated for difficulty by real match outcomes (Lichess puzzle co-evolution), and ranked by plays/quality. Injection-screened at publish. *Why:* UGC solves the 3-boss content starvation forever and gives creators a status loop; Steam-Workshop dynamics in a niche this identity-driven compound (ranking-systems: AutoIRT-style priors seed new-boss ratings). **L**

9. **Coach Mode (manager/mentor surface).** A mentor gets a link to review your persisted calls: timeline-scrub replay, judge annotations, ability to drop timestamped voice/text notes, and "assign this boss as homework." Free for 1 mentee; team tier for rosters. *Why:* converts TeamHub from 100% fake to the paid on-ramp; Quantified's documented failure is that enterprise reps won't voluntarily practice — a human accountability layer on top of a game they already like inverts that (competitors research). **M**

10. **Live Bracket Events ("One Shot World Championship").** Quarterly livestreamed single-elim bracket: qualifiers from the ranked ladder, influencer judges, same boss per round faced blind, every match auto-clipped, modest prize ($2–5K max — Hyperbound proved status beats prizes). Tournaments screen stops advertising fake events and hosts real ones. *Why:* PogChamps (375K peak concurrents) + the April 2025 Cold Calling Championship pre-validated "humans vs. AI buyer" as spectator content; each event is a step-function inbound moment (Hyperbound founders, on record). **L** (ops-heavy; run #1 at day ~75).

## 4. The Viral Engine: Five Loops

**Loop 1 — The Roast Grid (share-card).** Every One Shot (and any opted-in call) auto-generates a spoiler-free result artifact, Wordle-grammar: 10 rubric squares as colored emoji (🟩 4, 🟨 2–3, 🟥 0–1), outcome badge (`☠️ HUNG UP ON @ 0:47` / `🤝 CLOSED`), percentile, rank emblem, one savage roast line, and the challenge URL. Copy-paste text block for LinkedIn/Slack (native text beats links by 38% on LinkedIn) + PNG via @vercel/og for DMs. *Mechanics:* share button is the primary CTA on PostCall, pre-focused after One Shot; spoiler-free because everyone faces the same boss that week. *K contribution:* this is the cycle-time weapon — a weekly-to-daily share moment at ~1-day cycle time compounds faster than doubling invite volume (K-factor research: cycle time ≥ invite rate). Target attach rate >25% of One Shot completions.

**Loop 2 — The Carnage Clip.** Feature #2's pipeline, pointed at distribution: 15–30s vertical audiogram of the worst/best moment, captioned, watermarked with the challenge link. Post-call screen ranks your three most clippable moments and renders on demand. LinkedIn gets the static grid + a native-document "my week of roasts" recap (documents = 6.1% engagement, above video); TikTok/Shorts/Reels get the audiogram (vertical +58% mobile engagement). *Asymmetry rule:* the hang-up clip ships with one tap, the victory clip takes two — losing must travel further (negativity bias, 5x weight). Clips are also our own content pipeline: an official account posting the day's most brutal hang-up rides the documented "cold call with me" trend.

**Loop 3 — Challenge Links + Embeds.** Every boss, duel, and score has a canonical URL that cold-starts an anonymous playable session in <60s (no signup until the grade reveal). Company embed widgets seed leaderboards on third-party sites. *Mechanics:* clicking a friend's roast grid → same boss → your grade → your grid → signup wall to save it. Median referral conversion is 3–5% for passive invites; a playable challenge that delivers value before the wall should clear top-quartile 8%+ (K-factor research).

**Loop 4 — Referral = Extra One Shot Attempt.** One Shot's scarcity creates the only currency worth referring for: each converted signup grants one banked re-attempt token (usable any future week, cap 3 banked, never 2 in one week — scarcity must survive). HQ Trivia's uncapped extra-life referral is the direct precedent. Secondary earn: streak freezes. *Anti-abuse:* tokens grant only on referred account completing placements + Turnstile + device-cluster heuristics (ranking-systems integrity model).

**Loop 5 — Company Leaderboards as ambient pressure.** Domain-clustered ladders (feature #6) mean every player's result silently repositions their employer publicly. Weekly "Your company fell to #52 — Acme's SDRs passed you" digest email/notification; Wrapped-style seasonal "Your Sales Org, Roasted" company cards at Act end (Spotify Wrapped 2025: 200M engaged users, ~500M shares — the corporate-identity variant is unclaimed in sales). This loop recruits coworkers, the highest-conversion invite class, and feeds monetization directly.

**K math, honestly:** target K ≥ 0.3 at ~1–2 day cycle time (good consumer benchmark is 0.15–0.25; 0.4 is great). Composition: grid shares (high volume, low per-share conversion) + challenge links (low volume, high conversion) + referrals (deliberate). At K=0.3/1.5-day cycle, 1,000 seeded users → ~4,800 in 30 days before paid/influencer amplification; the influencer boss drops (Loop-adjacent) provide the periodic step functions Hyperbound demonstrated.

## 5. 90-Day Launch Calendar

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4). Build in quiet.**
- **W1:** Supabase auth + schema + RLS; port 6 endpoints server-side; kill localStorage Elo (one-time import at first login); fix the 5 trust bugs (§2.5); OG meta + real title. PostHog instrumented from day one.
- **W2:** Gemini Live voice arena (ephemeral tokens, 2.5-native-audio) + hang-up tool + ambience; sidecar sentiment/hints.
- **W3:** Glicko-2 engine + boss ratings + placements; persisted matches/transcripts/grades; deterministic-metrics panel; scoring pipeline v1 (ensemble + quote verification + golden set seeded from our own test calls).
- **W4:** One Shot mode (server-locked attempts) + roast grid share card + challenge links + anonymous demo boss; PostCall reveal polish. **Gate to Phase 2:** voice call P50 latency <1s, grade consistency (same transcript re-graded) within ±1 band per metric, share card renders. Private alpha: 30–50 reps recruited via founder DMs.

**Phase 2 — Seed (Weeks 5–8). Earn the niche.**
- **W5:** Soft launch. Founder-story post in **Bravado War Room** (400K members, anonymous culture = ideal for roast screenshots) and **r/sales** ("I built an AI that roasts your cold calls — it made me cry"; no links until asked — 90/10 rule). Daily One Shot cadence begins (weekly boss + daily arcade leaderboard). Watch D1 retention.
- **W6:** Clip pipeline ships. Official TikTok/Shorts account starts daily "AI destroys a real rep" clips riding the *cold call with me* trend. Duel-a-Friend ships. Begin influencer outreach: 10 targets from Demandbase Top 100 + 30MPC orbit (Farrokh/Cegelski, Kosoglow, Allen-Knuth, Coleman, Ingram, Aitken, Plowman) — offer: consented likeness boss, own leaderboard, rev-share on referred subs.
- **W7:** Referral-for-attempts + streaks + company leaderboards ship. Pitch **Sell Better Daily Sales Show** and **Cognism Cold Calling Live** appearances (both formats pre-validated humans-vs-AI content).
- **W8:** **First Influencer Boss Drop** (the biggest name secured), 2-week window, public leaderboard, they announce to their audience. **Gate to Phase 3:** ≥2,000 registered, D1 ≥ 50% (bar: LinkedIn games hit 84%), share attach ≥ 15%, K ≥ 0.15, grade-dispute rate <5% of calls.

**Phase 3 — Event (Weeks 9–13). Manufacture the moment.**
- **W9:** Announce the **One Shot World Championship** (bracket, influencer judges, $2–5K pool); qualifiers = ranked ladder + weekly One Shot percentiles. Boss Workshop opens in beta (UGC supply for season 2).
- **W10–11:** Qualifier weeks — every qualifier run auto-clipped; regional + company leaderboards promoted; second and third influencer boss drops timed as qualifier bosses. Coach Mode beta to managers who've appeared in company ladders (pipeline for §6).
- **W12:** **Finals livestream** (Sell Better-style partner co-promotes; PogChamps format) + **Product Hunt launch the same week** — PH is one coordinated day for investor/press attention (~800–1,200 upvotes for AI #1), not a strategy; stack it on the finals for compounding coverage.
- **W13:** "SalesWorx Wrapped — Season 1" personal + company stat cards pushed to every player; Season 2 / Act 1 begins with soft reset and peak-rank rewards. **Success bar at day 90:** 10K registered, ~2K MAU, D1 ≥ 60%, K ≥ 0.3, share attach ≥ 25%, infra cost within the $1.6–3.1K/mo envelope (infra research), ≥3 inbound team-tier requests.

## 6. Monetization That Doesn't Kill Virality

**Iron rule: everything that generates a share or a rank is free.** Ranked ladder, weekly One Shot, roast grid, clips, duels, company leaderboards, public profiles — free forever. The viral loops are the CAC engine; paywalling any of them starves it (Wordle/Duolingo lesson; Hyperbound's free demo is its whole funnel).

- **Free:** 2 voice calls/day + 10 text calls/day (worst-case ~$0.40/user/day at Gemini rates — infra research), 1 One Shot/week, full ranked + roast + shares, 3 saved replays.
- **Pro — $24/mo ($19 annual):** priced deliberately between Yoodli's $8 utility tier and SellMeThisPen $44/Kendo $55/SecondBody ~$45 (competitors verification). Sells **depth and volume, never rank**: unlimited voice minutes (the metered cost, hence the natural lever — infra research), deep feedback (full evidence quotes, line-by-line annotated replays, per-metric trend coaching, the judge's `improvements`/`rewrite` output), full replay vault + clip studio (custom cuts, no watermark), advanced Boss Workshop slots, priority One Shot regrade review. Pro buys you *better practice and better content about yourself* — a Pro player has zero ladder advantage, preserving competitive legitimacy (chess.com's exact model).
- **Team — $49/seat/mo, min 5, self-serve:** Coach Mode rosters, private team ladders + assigned homework bosses, manager analytics on real rubric aggregates, roast-intensity org pinning, private company bosses via the Workshop. Sold bottom-up: company leaderboards (free, public) create the manager demand; no sales team, no 2-week onboarding — the anti-Hyperbound wedge. Enterprise (SSO, SLAs) deferred until Teams pulls it.
- **Events/sponsorships (later):** branded influencer boss drops and tournament sponsorships (Orum/Sendoso-class sponsors already fund this exact format per Hyperbound's League of Sales Legends) — revenue that *adds* virality instead of taxing it.
- **Never sell:** rating boosts, extra One Shot attempts for money (referral-only — paying for attempts collapses both the scarcity and the referral loop), or removal of the roast. The roast is the brand; the ladder is the trust; the share is the growth. Price everything else.
